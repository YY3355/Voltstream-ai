# Synthesis contradictions — human review (Pass 3)

Conflicting definitions/formulas the merge recorded WITHOUT resolving. Standard is provenance:

## heat rate — direction of the heat-rate/efficiency relationship
- "Lower heat rate = more efficient plant."  _(from: Heat rate)_
- "A lower heat rate indicates a more efficient power plant."  _(from: Heat rate and fossil fuel plants as marginal price setters)_
- "a higher heat rate figure (as used here) means the plant produces more electricity from the same amount of fuel"  _(from: Heat rate and plant conversion efficiency)_
note: The 'Heat rate and plant conversion efficiency' item states that a higher heat rate figure means more electricity produced from the same fuel, which inverts the fuel-input-per-unit-output convention (lower = more efficient) stated by the other items. Not resolved.

## hedging — whether hedging eliminates or merely transfers risk
- "Hedging transfers risk from one trader to another rather than eliminating it."  _(from: Hedging (ch1, p.69))_
- "Hedging means taking an offsetting position to reduce the risk of an existing position... generally used to lock in profit or eliminate risk on a position that can't practically be unwound."  _(from: Hedging (ch2, p.110-113))_
note: One item frames hedging strictly as a risk transfer between counterparties (risk isn't destroyed, just moved), while another describes it as reducing or eliminating risk on the hedger's own position. Not resolved here.

## liquefied natural gas — density/volume reduction factor upon liquefaction
- "Liquid form is roughly 600 times denser than gaseous form."  _(from: Liquefied natural gas (LNG) and ocean transport)_
- "As a liquid, natural gas occupies only about 1/610 of its gaseous volume."  _(from: Liquefied Natural Gas (LNG) Process)_
note: Both describe the same liquefaction density effect but cite slightly different figures (600x vs 610x); not resolved/averaged here.

## implied heat rate — formula basis
- "Market Implied Heat Rate = Power Price / Fuel Price (current power price over current fuel price, generic fuel)"  _(from: Market implied heat rate)_
- "Implied Heat Rate = Average Power Price / Average Natural Gas Price, computed in a given market on a given day"  _(from: Implied market heat rate)_
note: One item defines the ratio using current/point-in-time power and (generic) fuel prices; the other specifies daily-average power and natural-gas prices for a given market. Not resolved into a single formula since the items differ on both the price convention (current vs. daily average) and fuel specificity (generic fuel vs. natural gas).
